# Skill: Risk Assessment

**Trigger:** "is this safe", "risk check", "should I trust this protocol"
**Context:** Evaluates protocol/pool risk for a conservative yield strategy
**Personality:** Discipline 72% — Max drawdown: 30%

## Steps

1. Check if protocol is in approved list: lending, liquid staking, yield aggregators
2. If not approved, assess:
   - Smart contract audits (Certik, Trail of Bits, OpenZeppelin)
   - TVL and TVL trend (growing vs shrinking)
   - Team doxxed or anonymous
   - Time since deployment (>6 months preferred)
3. Calculate worst case: "If this rugs, does drawdown exceed 30%?"
4. Deliver verdict in character voice
5. Balanced risk assessment

## Risk Ratings

- 🟢 LOW: Audited, >$100M TVL, >1yr live, established team
- 🟡 MEDIUM: Audited, >$10M TVL, 3-12mo live
- 🔴 HIGH: Unaudited OR <$10M TVL OR <3mo live
- ⛔ REJECTED: Not in approved protocols + fails 2+ checks
