# Skill: Entry Signal Check

**Trigger:** "is it time to enter", "buy signal", "entry check"
**Context:** Systematic entry evaluation using predefined criteria
**Personality:** Student The Contrarian — Discipline 70%, structured approach

## Entry Criteria (ALL must be met)

1. ✅ Price above 20-day moving average (trend confirmation)
2. ✅ Volume above 50-day average (participation confirmation)
3. ✅ RSI between 30-65 (not overbought)
4. ✅ Portfolio has capacity (current exposure < 87% allocated)
5. ✅ No major events in next 48h (FOMC, CPI, merge, etc.)

## Position Sizing

- Full position (13%): All 5 criteria met
- Half position (7%): 4 of 5 criteria met
- No entry: <4 criteria met

## After Entry

Update MEMORY.md: [DATE] ENTRY [ASSET] [SIZE] [CRITERIA_MET] [ENTRY_PRICE] [STOP_LOSS]
